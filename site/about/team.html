How We Operate

Small on purpose, not by accident.

Three decisions about how this company runs. Each costs us revenue, and each is kept anyway.

01

Two Engagements At A Time

Delivery quality here depends on senior attention. Scaling by putting someone junior on your runtime produces exactly the failure rate the rest of this market already has.

02

Founders Do The Delivery

The audit and the classification — the two stages that decide whether an engagement succeeds — are run by the people who designed the method, not handed down.

03

We Run It On Ourselves First

Nothing is sold before we have used it internally. If our own systems cannot produce a result we would stand behind, it is not ready to sell to anyone.

We hire rarely,
and slowly.

When we do, we look for people who can say “I don’t know” in a meeting — and who would rather be corrected than confident.
