The Limits

What we don’t do.

Most companies publish what they can do. This page exists because being clear about the opposite protects both sides. An engagement that should never have started is worse for us than one we never won.

On The Product

What the control plane cannot do for you.

Every security control has a boundary. These are ours.

Limit 01

We Don’t Detect Attacks

Prompt injection classifiers, content filters and anomaly scoring are not what this is. We remove capability. If you want detection as well, keep the layer you have — we assume it will miss things.

Limit 02

We Can’t Protect A Compromised Host

The threat model assumes the gateway host is trusted. If an attacker has root on the machine running the control plane, the control plane is not your problem.

Limit 03

We Can’t Fix A Bad Agent

If your agent produces wrong answers, enforcement does not make them right. It bounds what the wrong answer can cause. Those are different problems.

Limit 04

Cold Start Is Real

A new agent with no observed behaviour has no learned policy. Shadow mode delivers the audit immediately, but enforcement waits for a confidence threshold. We will not enforce a guess.

On Services

Work we turn down.

Each of these has cost us revenue.

01

We don’t sell zero-human operation

Every system we build requires a named human owner with authority to intervene. Legal accountability requires a person. Anyone promising a fully autonomous department is describing something they cannot deliver safely.

02

We decline engagements below a sixty percent ceiling

If the honest autonomy ceiling for a department does not justify the cost of transforming it, we say so before you spend. A client who pays for a transformation that cannot deliver becomes a reference against us.

03

We don’t run multi-year programmes

Company-wide, multi-year change belongs to large systems integrators with the delivery scale for it. We do one department at a time, with a fixed scope and a fixed price.

04

We don’t provide legal advice

Our compliance work identifies which regulations apply and what obligations follow. It never produces the legal instrument that satisfies them. Where formal advice is required we coordinate qualified professionals rather than substituting for them.

05

We don’t take more than two engagements at a time

Delivery quality here depends on senior attention. If we are engaged, we will tell you when we can start rather than starting badly.

06

We don’t sell agent-building tooling

We govern agents and we build bounded systems. We are not a platform for other people to build agents on, and we do not compete with the frameworks you already use.

Not A Fit

Who we are not for.

Naming this saves everyone a wasted conversation.

Not a fitWhy
Teams wanting a detection productWe remove capability rather than recognise attacks. If detection is the requirement, we are the wrong layer.
Organisations seeking zero-human operationWe do not sell it, and we will correct the expectation rather than take the engagement.
Companies without documented exception rulesAutomation requires knowing what the exceptions are. Without them written down, the honest ceiling is very low.
Anyone needing enforcement on day oneShadow mode runs first. A policy enforced before it is learned is a policy that breaks your agents.
Buyers requiring SOC 2 todayWe do not hold it yet. We will not claim a certification we have not been audited for.
Multi-year transformation programmesFour people. We say so rather than discovering it together in month four.

On What We Claim

Things you won’t find on this website.

A company built on published error rates cannot decorate its own site with numbers it cannot evidence.

Absent 01

Accuracy Percentages

No “94% accurate” claim. There is no independent benchmark in this category and a self-graded number means nothing.

Absent 02

Customer Counts

No “500+ companies protected.” When we publish numbers they will be ones you could ask us to substantiate.

Absent 03

Testimonials And Logos

No quotes, no client logos, no partner badges until they are real, current, and used with permission.

Absent 04

“World’s First” Anything

Unverifiable superlatives are the fastest way to lose a technical buyer. We describe what the system does instead.

Absent 05

Certifications We Don’t Hold

No SOC 2 or ISO badges until they are independently audited and current. Security claims we make are ones we can demonstrate.

Absent 06

Case Studies Without Clients

The case studies page stays empty until two real engagements are published with permission. It is in the navigation so you can see that it is empty.

When our calibration record exists — detection rates, false positives, and where we were wrong — it will be published in full on its own page. Until then, this page is what we have instead of statistics.

If any of this rules us out,
that’s the page working.

And if it does not, you now know exactly what you are buying.
