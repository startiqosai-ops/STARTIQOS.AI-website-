Privacy Policy

What we collect, what we do not, and what happens to it. The short version: the control plane runs in your environment and your data does not reach us. Last updated 14 September 2026.

What the control plane collects

The control plane records argument shapes and types, never argument values. A trace records that a query had a table name and a row limit within a range. It does not record which table or which rows.

This is a schema decision made at the start, not a redaction step applied afterwards. It cannot be reversed, and it is the reason traces are anonymisable by construction.

Where it is stored

Traces and the attestation ledger stay inside your environment. The control plane is self-hosted; there is no inbound connection from us and no dependency on our availability.

Where you opt into the shared profile library, only anonymised aggregate metadata syncs outward — task-class names and capability frequencies, never traces.

What this website collects

Essential cookies to make the site function. Optional analytics to understand which pages are read, which you can decline.

If you submit the contact form we receive your name, email, company and message. We use it to reply to you. We do not add you to a marketing list, because we do not operate one.

Sub-processors

For the website: our hosting provider and, where you consent, an analytics provider.

For engagements: named in the engagement agreement before work begins, with their data handling terms attached. We do not add a sub-processor mid-engagement without telling you.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act and, where applicable, the GDPR, you may request access to personal data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted.

Write to info@startiqosai.com. We respond within thirty days.

Grievance officer

A named grievance officer is appointed as required under section 13 of the DPDP Act. Contact details are published here before we begin processing personal data for any client.
